Questions

Anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

Valeting is a quick, regular clean. Detailing goes further, with decontamination, machine polishing and protection that restore the finish and keep it. Our Safe Wash and Heritage Express are regular cleans. The Heritage Reset and everything above it are full details.

Small covers cars like a Polo, Audi A1, Corsa or MINI. Medium covers a Golf, Audi A3, Focus or Ibiza. Large covers a BMW 5 Series, Audi A6 or E-Class. XL / Prestige covers a BMW X5, Range Rover, Audi Q7, Porsche or RS models. Not sure? Tell us the make and model and we’ll confirm.

It depends on the service. A Safe Wash takes around an hour, a Heritage Reset around three hours and Interior Enrichment around six. Paint correction and ceramic coating take one to three days. We’ll confirm timings with your quote.

Condition varies from car to car, so every price is finalised after a physical inspection. Excessive dirt, mud, pet hair or sand can mean extra time and an additional charge.

Send a quote request or give us a call. A 20% deposit secures your date, and it can be moved to a new booking with at least 48 hours’ notice. The balance is due when the work is finished, by card or bank transfer.
