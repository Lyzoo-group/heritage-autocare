How ceramic coating protects your vehicle

A hard, clear shell over your paint.

A ceramic coating cures hard on top of your clear coat and becomes the outer layer of your paintwork. UV, bird lime, road salt and washing wear on the coating, not on the paint underneath.

What it does

  • Deepens gloss and colour
  • Makes water bead and run off
  • Lets dirt release more easily when you wash
  • Resists UV, bird lime and winter road salt

Applied after a full clean and polish, using professional products from Gyeon, Koch Chemie, Gtechniq and Carpro.

How we coat your car

Three stages, all carried out in our studio at Enterprise Park.

  1. Prepare

    A safe wash, then a full chemical decontamination to remove tar, iron fallout and old wax, so nothing gets sealed under the coating.

    Safe wash, chemical decontamination

  2. Polish

    A single stage machine polish lifts light swirls and brings back clarity. Every panel is checked under the studio's hexagon lighting.

    Single stage machine polish

  3. Protect

    A panel wipe removes polishing oils, then the three-year ceramic coating goes on panel by panel. It is cured and finished before we hand back the keys.

    Panel wipe, coating, curing and finishing
