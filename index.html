Car detailing from a one-hour safe wash to two-stage paint correction, ceramic coatings, paint protection film and add-ons. We also work with trade and fleet clients.

Prices depend on your vehicle’s size and condition. Detailing starts from £35, ceramic coating from £495 and paint protection film from £750. Every price is confirmed after we’ve inspected your car.

Our studio is at Unit 11, Enterprise Park, 147 Drakemire Drive, Glasgow G45 9EE. We’re open Monday to Friday, 10am to 6pm.

It depends on the service. A Safe Wash takes around an hour and a Heritage Reset around three hours. Paint correction and ceramic coating take one to three days. We’ll confirm timings with your quote.

Send a quote request or give us a call. A 20% deposit secures your date, and it can be moved to a new booking with at least 48 hours’ notice. The balance is due when the work is finished, by card or bank transfer.
