What is professional car detailing?

Professional car detailing is a thorough clean and finish of a car’s interior and exterior using specialist products and careful technique. Where a standard car wash removes surface dirt, detailing aims to restore the look of every surface and prepare it for protection.

01

Exterior car detailing

It starts with a safe wash, using pH-safe products and careful technique to lift dirt without marking the paint. Decontamination then removes the bonded grime and a wash can’t shift. Wheels, tyres and glass are cleaned, and a finishing step brings out the gloss.

02

Interior car detailing

Car interior detailing means cleaning the cabin properly, not simply tidying it. Our Interior Enrichment service is a comprehensive deep clean designed to bring the cabin as close to factory condition as possible, which suits neglected cabins and busy family cars.

03

Paint enhancement

If the paint looks dull or swirled, machine polishing can improve gloss and reduce light defects. We offer an Enhancement Detail and one- and two-stage paint correction, explained in full on our paint correction page.

New car detailing in Glasgow

A new car isn’t always a finished one. Between the factory, transport and the showroom, it can pick up light defects, contamination and wash marks before you’ve driven a mile. Our New Car Detail is designed to put that right before you start using the car.

Preparation matters most when you plan to protect the paint. Coatings and film go on over the paintwork, so it makes sense to start with it clean and in good condition. After your New Car Detail, you can add ceramic coating, paint protection film, or both.
