Ceramic paint protection

A hard, clear shell over your paint

An auto ceramic coating, or ceramic for cars, is a protective coating for cars that cures hard on top of your clear coat and becomes the outer layer of your paintwork. UV, bird lime, road salt and washing wear on the coating, not on the paint underneath. It is the longest-lasting car paint protection coating short of film, and true ceramic protection for car paintwork, not a wax.

  • Deeper glossRicher colour and a glass-like finish.
  • Water beads offRain runs off and takes dirt with it.
  • Easier washingGrime releases with a gentle wash.
  • Winter-readyResists road salt, UV and bird lime.

Car detailing ceramic coating

How we apply a professional ceramic coating

Three stages, all carried out in our studio at Enterprise Park. A coating is only as good as the paint underneath it, so every ceramic coating starts with proper detailing.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    A safe wash, then a full chemical decontamination to remove tar, iron fallout and old wax, so nothing gets sealed under the coating.

  2. 02

    Polish

    A single stage machine polish lifts light swirls and brings back clarity. Every panel is checked under the studio's hexagon lighting.

  3. 03

    Protect

    A panel wipe removes polishing oils, then the three-year ceramic coating goes on panel by panel. It is cured and finished before we hand back the keys.

    What is the best ceramic coating for cars?+

    The preparation matters more than the bottle. A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, so the best auto ceramic coating is one applied to properly decontaminated and polished paint, in a studio, by trained detailers. That is why the best professional ceramic coating for cars always starts with a proper detail.

    Do you use Gtechniq ceramic coating?+

    Yes. Gtechniq, often searched as gtech ceramic coating or g tech ceramic coating, is one of the professional brands we work with, alongside Gyeon, Carpro and Koch Chemie. If you have a coating in mind, tell us in your quote request and we'll confirm the product for your car.

    Is ceramic better than other protective coatings for cars?+

    Wax and sealants sit on the paint and wear off in weeks or months. A ceramic coating bonds to the clear coat and lasts for years, with harder, glossier protection and much easier washing.
