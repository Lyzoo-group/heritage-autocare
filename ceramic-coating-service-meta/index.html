How it works

8 simple steps to a new-looking car

Here’s exactly what happens to your car in our studio.

  1. 01

    Safe car wash

    We wash your car gently with soft foam, so it gets clean without scratches.

  2. 02

    Deep clean the paint

    Special sprays melt away tiny stuck-on dirt that washing can’t remove.

  3. 03

    Paint correction

    Fixes deeper scratches and marks, if your car needs it.

    Optional extra
  4. 04

    Paint protection

    An extra clear layer for the parts that get hit most, like the front.

    Optional extra
  5. 05

    Machine polish

    A polishing machine takes away light swirls and brings back the shine.

  6. 06

    Panel wipe

    We wipe every panel so the paint is perfectly clean and ready.

  7. 07

    Ceramic coating

    We lock in the shine with a ceramic layer that protects your paint for 3 years.

  8. 08

    Curing and finishing

    Your car rests indoors while the coating hardens, then gets a final check.
