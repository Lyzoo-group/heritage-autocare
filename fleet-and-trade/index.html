Services for business vehicles

Every Heritage service is available to fleet and trade customers, priced on quote for your vehicles.

Keep clean

Regular care for vehicles in daily use.

  • Safe Wash

    Studio-grade exterior wash with pH-safe products and contact-safe techniques.

  • Heritage Express

    A fast inside and out makeover for vehicles in daily use.

  • Heritage Reset

    A thorough clean inside and out, finished with gloss-enhancing products.

  • Interior Enrichment

    A deep interior clean that brings the cabin as close to factory condition as possible.

Prepare for sale

Presentation work for stock and returning vehicles.

  • Sales Preparation Package

    Enhancement Detail and Interior Enrichment together, to maximise visual appeal inside and out.

  • Enhancement Detail

    A single-stage machine polish that improves gloss and reduces light defects.

  • Paint correction

    One or two machine polishing stages for swirl marks, light scratches and oxidation.

  • Add-ons

    Engine bay detailing, odour removal, headlight restoration and more.

Protect

Long-term protection for new and high-value vehicles.

  • New Car Detail

    Decontamination, a light machine polish and 3 year protection for cars straight from the factory.

  • Ceramic Protection Package

    A single-stage machine polish and a 3 year professional ceramic coating.

  • Paint protection film

    Self-healing film over the panels most exposed to stone chips.

  • Wheel and arch ceramic

    Wheels off, detailed and ceramic coated, with the arches steam cleaned.
